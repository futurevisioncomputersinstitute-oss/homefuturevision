Quick Facts

Who is Future Vision?

Future Vision Computer Institute is a Surat-based computer and AI training institute, founded in 2006 by Siddharth Parakh. It has trained more than 5,000 students over 20+ years.

Where is it located?

Future Vision operates 3 branches in Surat, Gujarat — Citylight, Vesu and Pal. Full addresses are listed below.

What does it specialize in?

Computer fundamentals, coding (Python, C, C++), data analytics & business intelligence, computer accounting (Tally, Zoho Books), graphic design, and digital marketing — with Generative AI and Agentic AI integrated across its course catalogue.

Who teaches there?

Courses are taught by in-house trainers led by founder Siddharth Parakh, including specialists such as Yash Jain (Data & Analytics). Full faculty details are being added to this page.

How long has it existed?

Since 2006 — 20+ years of continuous operation in Surat.

Who is it suitable for?

Students, working professionals and career-switchers looking for practical, job-ready training — from complete beginners (Computer Basics & Gen AI Foundation) to advanced, placement-focused certificate programs.
