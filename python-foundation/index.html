FAQ

We assume you have questions

Do I need any prior coding experience to join? +

Not at all. The Python Foundations Program starts from absolute basics — you only need basic computer knowledge and a willingness to practice consistently (about 1 hour of self-study a day).

How long is the program? +

It runs over 1.5 months (6 weeks) with 36+ learning hours — 100% hands-on practical coding, plus specialized career coaching sessions.

Who is this program ideal for? +

Complete beginners, BCA / BSc / Commerce students, and career switchers who want a solid, structured entry into programming and the IT industry.

Will I actually write code, or just theory? +

You code from day one. Every concept is applied in small, confidence-building programs like a calculator, number-guessing game, quiz app and a file-based contact book.

Will I get a certificate? +

Yes — a Python Foundations Program certificate on completion to strengthen your resume, LinkedIn and further studies.

What can I do after this course? +

You will be ready for entry-level roles like Junior Python Developer or Automation Executive — and to advance into Web Development, Data Science or AI programs.
