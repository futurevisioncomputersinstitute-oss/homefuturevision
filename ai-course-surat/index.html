AI course in Surat: which one do you actually need?

We run three. They cost ₹6,000, ₹7,200 and ₹77,000, and the difference between them is not quality — it is what you walk out able to do. This page helps you pick before you pay.

Citylight · Vesu · Pal · Updated August 2026

Short answer. If you are new to computers, take the six-week Computer Basics & Gen AI Foundation (₹6,000). If you already work in an office and want AI to make you faster, take Professional Office & Generative AI (two months, ₹7,200). If you want a developer job building AI systems, take the Advanced Certificate in Python & Agentic AI (eleven months, ₹77,000).

Only the third is a career program. The first two are skill courses that make your existing work faster. Anyone who tells you a six-week AI course leads to a job is selling you something.

Answer three questions and the choice makes itself

1. Are you comfortable with a computer already?

Not “can you use WhatsApp” — can you manage files, folders, email attachments and a spreadsheet without help? If the honest answer is no, start with the foundation course. Every AI tool sits on top of ordinary computer skills, and people who skip that step spend the whole AI class stuck on things that have nothing to do with AI.

2. Do you want to use AI or build it?

This is the real fork, and it decides ₹7,200 versus ₹77,000.

Using AI means writing better prompts, checking output for errors, and folding tools into work you already do — drafting, summarising, cleaning up data, making slides. It is a writing and judgement skill. No code.

Building AI means writing Python that calls models, chains steps together, handles failures and runs without a person watching. That is a software developer’s job, and eleven months is what it honestly takes from a standing start.

3. How much time do you actually have?

The two short courses fit around a job. The eleven-month certificate does not run on class hours alone — it needs practice between sessions. If you cannot find that time, take a shorter course now and come back later. That is a better outcome than paying ₹77,000 and falling behind in month four.

Things worth saying plainly about AI courses

A certificate is not what gets you hired

Employers in Surat asking for AI skills want to see something you built or something you made faster. Our longest course is built around project work for exactly this reason. Treat any certificate — ours included — as proof you turned up, not proof you can do the work.

The tools change; the thinking does not

The specific AI products in use today will look different in two years. What survives is knowing how to break a task into steps a machine can do, how to tell a good answer from a confident wrong one, and where automation is a bad idea. We teach the tools currently in use, because you need something concrete to practise on, but the transferable part is the judgement.

AI does not replace the underlying subject

An AI tool will write you a formula, but you still need to know whether the number it produced is right. This is why our AI teaching sits inside real subjects — Office work, Python, data — instead of being a standalone “AI course” with nothing underneath it.

Free tools are a real option, and sometimes the right one

If you have never tried ChatGPT or Claude on your own work, do that first. It is free and it will tell you a lot about whether you need a course. People who benefit most from ours are the ones who have already hit the limits of poking around alone.

Common questions

Which AI course is best for a complete beginner in Surat?

Computer Basics and Gen AI Foundation, six weeks, Rs 6,000. It assumes you have never used a computer seriously before. You learn the machine and the AI tools together, so you are not spending two months on typing practice before touching anything modern.

Do I need to know coding to learn AI?

Not for two of our three courses. Using AI tools well is a writing and thinking skill, not a programming skill. Coding becomes necessary only when you want to build AI systems rather than use them, which is what the Python and Agentic AI certificate covers.

How much does an AI course cost in Surat?

Ours run from Rs 6,000 for the six-week foundation to Rs 77,000 for the eleven-month Agentic AI certificate. The gap is large because they are genuinely different things. One teaches you to use AI tools, the other trains you for a developer job.

Will an AI course get me a job?

A short AI course will not, on its own. It makes you faster at a job you already do or are already qualified for. The eleven-month Agentic AI certificate is the one built as a career program, and even then what gets people hired is the project work, not the certificate.

Is ChatGPT enough, or do I need a course?

If you only want to ask questions and get answers, you do not need a course. People come to us when the free tools stopped being enough: they need reliable output for work, they need to know when the answer is wrong, or they need to connect AI to real files and real data.

How long is the Agentic AI course and what does it cover?

Eleven months. It covers Python from the ground up, then LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI and Claude for building agent systems. It is an advanced program and the longest course we run in any subject.

Do you teach AI at all three Surat branches?

Yes. Citylight, Vesu and Pal all run the AI courses. Batch timings differ by branch, so call before you travel if a specific slot matters to you.

Can I learn AI while working full time?

Yes, and most of our AI students do. The two shorter courses are built around working schedules. The eleven-month certificate is demanding and needs practice time outside class, so be honest with yourself about your hours before enrolling in that one.
