Premiere Pro and After Effects do different jobs

This is the question most people arrive with, so here it is plainly:

Premiere ProAfter Effects
What it doesAssembles footage you filmedCreates things never filmed
Used forCutting, sequencing, audio, colour, exportTitles, motion graphics, visual effects, animation
Think of it asThe editThe graphics
Learn itFirstAfter Premiere

A real job usually uses both: the shot sequence is built in Premiere, the animated title is made in After Effects and brought back in. Editors learn Premiere first, and most editing work only ever needs Premiere.

What the course covers

Premiere Pro — the edit

  • Importing and organising footage, working with sequences and nested sequences
  • Cutting on the timeline — the actual craft of where a cut goes
  • Multi-camera editing and syncing clips from several cameras
  • Transitions — and why most of them should not be used
  • Audio: levels, effects, music and sound design
  • Colour correction and grading
  • Titles, shapes and logos in the frame
  • Captions and closed captions
  • Export settings for YouTube, Instagram and client delivery

After Effects — the graphics

  • Motion titles and animated lower thirds
  • Motion tracking
  • Effects, transitions and templates
  • Expressions and scripts for repeatable animation
  • Basic 3D animation

Short form — reels and UGC ads

The part that pays soonest. Vertical editing for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, pacing for a feed rather than a screen, hooks in the first second, and UGC ad creatives — short videos made to look like a customer filmed them on a phone rather than like an advertisement, because that style performs better in feeds. Brands pay for these regularly, and it is some of the most available freelance video work in India right now.

The software is the easy half

You can learn the Premiere Pro interface in a fortnight. Anyone can.

What takes longer is knowing where to cut. How long to hold a shot before it goes dead. How sound carries a scene that the picture cannot. How to build pace so somebody watches to the end. That is judgement, and it comes from editing repeatedly with someone reviewing what you made and telling you why the third cut is a beat too late.

That is why video editing here sits inside a four or nine month course rather than a three-week software class, and why you leave with finished work rather than a certificate.

What computer you need

For the course, none. You work on our machines during class.

For practice at home, video is the most demanding thing most people ask of a computer. A practical minimum for comfortable 1080p editing: a recent multi-core processor, 16GB of RAM, an SSD, and a dedicated graphics card. Less will run Premiere Pro, but you will spend a lot of time waiting — and waiting is what stops people practising.

Common questions

What is the difference between Premiere Pro and After Effects?

Premiere Pro assembles footage: you cut clips, arrange them on a timeline, fix the audio and colour, and export the finished video. After Effects creates things that were never filmed: titles, motion graphics, visual effects and animation. A typical job uses both, with the shot sequence built in Premiere and the animated titles or effects made in After Effects and brought back in. Editors learn Premiere first.

How long does it take to learn video editing?

You can make a watchable cut within a fortnight. Editing well takes longer, because the software is the easy half. Knowing where to cut, how long to hold a shot, how sound carries a scene and how pace holds attention is craft rather than clicking, and it develops over months of doing it with someone reviewing your work. At Future Vision video editing sits inside four-month and nine-month courses for that reason.

What is a UGC ad creative?

UGC stands for user-generated content. A UGC ad is a short video made to look like an ordinary customer filmed it on a phone rather than like a produced advertisement, because that style performs better on Instagram and Facebook feeds. Brands pay editors to make them, and it is one of the steadiest sources of freelance video work in India right now. It is part of the four-month Digital Designing track.
